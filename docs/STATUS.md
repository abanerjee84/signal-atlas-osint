## Data and operational limits

The first full collection pass fetched 1,876 USGS records and 1,739 CISA records, with 1,000-record bounds. One USGS record was rejected. Retention removed older CISA events after normalization, so the database's retained total is lower than cumulative stored counts. Counts and current errors belong in `/api/status`, not hard-coded UI metrics.

Country boundaries are illustrative 1:110m Natural Earth geometry. Country-title mentions produce regional context, not precise incident positions. Missing coordinates remain missing. Playback reveals retained observations by the selected time basis, does not interpolate aircraft/vessel tracks and does not reconstruct deleted history.

All generative requests use OpenRouter. No inference credentials, synthetic world incidents, local LLM, paid feed, React, Docker, Redis or Celery were introduced. The generic maritime endpoint and additional RSS definitions are configuration hooks; placeholder examples do not produce live records.
